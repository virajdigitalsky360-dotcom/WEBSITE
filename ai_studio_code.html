// Post-Production Protocol

The Healthcare Editorial Pipeline

How raw clinic footage is securely handled, edited with anatomical precision, and delivered on schedule.

01

Encrypted Ingestion

Footage transfer via end-to-end encrypted medical drives or secure cloud. Patient confidentiality protocols strictly checked prior to timeline assembly.

02

Clinical Verification

Transcription review to ensure specialized terminology, dosage references, and procedure names are spelled accurately in all subtitles and lower-thirds.

03

Surgical Pacing & Grade

Removing sterile monotony while maintaining poise. Color-grading harsh blue/green hospital light back to natural skin and clinical tones.

04

Practice Master Delivery

Exporting multi-format deliverables (9:16 vertical, 16:9 4K, 1:1) with burned-in compliant captions and clean master files ready for broadcast or social.
